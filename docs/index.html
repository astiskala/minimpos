Free and open source · for Adyen Android terminals

The whole checkout, on the payment terminal.

Mini mPOS turns an Adyen Android terminal into a complete little point of sale. Ring up products, take the card payment, print or email the receipt, and refund by scanning it. No extra tablet, no cash register and no app subscription.

Get started View on GitHub
  • Runs on Android 9+ terminals
  • Any currency Adyen processes
  • Built-in simulator to try it out

Everything a small shop needs

Designed for cafés, market stalls, pop-ups and service businesses that want to keep things simple.

Products at your fingertips

Keep a product list with prices, categories, barcodes and tax. Search, filter, scan a barcode, or key in a custom amount.

Card payments done right

Payments go straight to the terminal's Adyen payment app. Every sale is saved first, and lost results are checked with the terminal automatically.

Receipts your way

One printed slip with your business details, the items, tax, the card receipt and a refund QR code. Or email it over your own mail server.

Refunds in seconds

Scan the QR code on the receipt, then refund everything, a few items, or an amount. Always linked to the original payment.

Pre-authorize deposits

Hold a deposit, booking or hire bond on the card with its own Pre-authorize tile. Capture or adjust it later from history, or cancel it.

Tips on the receipt

Print the receipt with lines for a tip and a signature, then enter what the customer wrote. The app captures bill plus tip, following Adyen's tipping on the receipt flow.

Tax and currency, the local way

Name your rates GST, VAT, SST, Consumption tax or Sales tax, with or without tax in the price. Take payments in any of the 138 currencies Adyen processes.

Set up the next terminal by QR

Copy products, settings and keys to another terminal by pointing its camera at a few QR codes. Passwords stay protected by a one-time transfer code.

Locked down

An admin PIN protects settings and products. Keys and passwords are encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Built for Adyen terminals

Mini mPOS talks to the terminal's own Adyen payment app through the local Terminal API, using Adyen's official Java library.

  • Secure by design. Messages are encrypted with your shared key, and the terminal's certificate is checked against Adyen's root certificates.
  • Card data stays with Adyen. The app only sees the card brand, masked number and references.
  • Follows Adyen's guidance. It meets the Android app requirements, sends application info, and handles declines, timeouts and busy terminals the way Adyen recommends.
  • Fits every screen. Down to the AMS1's 4-inch display, the main action stays in reach without scrolling.
  • No backend to run. Products, settings and history stay on the terminal. An optional Checkout API key lets the app capture tips and pre-authorizations itself.

Get started

Try it on your computer

No terminal needed: on an emulator or Android phone the app uses its built-in simulator.

git clone https://github.com/astiskala/minimpos.git
cd minimpos
./gradlew :app:installDebug

Put it on your terminals

  1. Create a shared key in your Adyen Customer Area.
  2. Download the signed APK from the latest release (or build your own) and upload it to Adyen.
  3. Deploy it to your terminals from the Customer Area.
  4. Enter the shared key in Mini mPOS and tap Save and test. Everything else is detected.
  5. Set up further terminals from the first one with a few QR codes.

Questions

Which terminals does it run on?

Adyen Android payment terminals running Android 9 or later, such as the S1F2, S1F4Pro, S1E4Pro, S1E2L, AMS1, S1U2 and SFO1. Printing needs a model with a printer, and QR scanning needs a camera.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Mini mPOS is free and open source under the MIT license. You pay Adyen for payments as usual.

Is it an Adyen product?

No. It is an independent project built for Adyen terminals. It isn't made, endorsed or supported by Adyen, so test it in your Adyen TEST environment before going live.

Can it take deposits or pre-authorizations?

Yes. Mark a product as a pre-authorization product and Home gets a Pre-authorize tile. The amount is only held on the card (with manual capture). With a Checkout API key you capture or adjust it from history; without one you capture it in the Customer Area. Either way you can cancel it from history. See Pre-authorizations in the guide.

Can customers add a tip?

Yes, on the receipt, on terminals with a printer. Switch on Tip on the receipt at checkout: the receipt prints with blank Tip and Total lines (and a signature line on the merchant copy), and once the customer has filled it in you enter the tip or the total. See Tips on the receipt in the guide.

Where is my data stored?

On the terminal. Products, settings and sales history never leave it, except for receipts you email through your own mail server.
