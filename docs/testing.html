Validate your TEST setup

  1. In Settings › Terminal, check that the environment is TEST. Complete the final Test connection step to verify API access and supported connection checks.
    • Physical terminal: run Test connection and check it passes.
    • Tap to Pay: confirm the phone is registered in Adyen Payments Test. The connection check validates setup only: there is no terminal diagnosis. Use the test payment below to check payment behavior.
  2. In Settings › Payments, check the currency. In Settings › Tax, check the rates and whether prices include tax.
  3. On Home, tap New sale, add a Custom item and tap Charge. Name the custom item “Test sale” and enter the amount for your test-card scenario.
  4. Tap Pay and follow the Adyen POS testing guide for a successful payment.
  5. Check that the result is approved and the receipt has the correct amount, currency and tax. Open the sale in History. Match its PSP reference to the payment in the TEST Customer Area.
  6. Refund the payment and check the final refund outcome in the TEST Customer Area. Refund requested is not final confirmation.
  7. Repeat the sale with a refused-payment scenario from the Adyen POS testing guide. Check that Mini mPOS shows a decline, not an approval; see declined payments .

Warning

If the result is unknown, do not pay again. Follow the recovery steps first.

Prepare your business for LIVE

  • Check currency and tax before setting product prices.
  • Check business details and test each receipt delivery method you will use.
  • Set staff PINs and check the actions that need Manager approval.
  • Enable and test only the optional payment workflows you intend to use.

Go live

Warning

Switching between TEST, LIVE and the simulator deletes all payment history on the device, including recovery data for unfinished payments. Resolve every payment under Needs attention first.

  1. Switch to the LIVE terminal or the LIVE Payments app, with LIVE credentials. For a network/cloud terminal or Tap to Pay, choose LIVE under Environment. Enter the Live URL prefix; see the Adyen live endpoints guide. Tap to Pay needs registration again in the selected environment.
  2. In Settings › Terminal, check that the environment is LIVE. Complete the final Test connection step to verify API access and supported connection checks.
    • Physical terminal: run Test connection and check it passes.
    • Tap to Pay: confirm the phone is registered in the LIVE Adyen Payments app. The connection check validates setup only: there is no terminal diagnosis.

For daily checks and reconciliation, see Close the day.

Verify your first LIVE payment

Follow Adyen’s live-testing guidance with your actual destination and receipt delivery method. Check the amount, currency, receipt and payment in the LIVE Customer Area. A connection check alone does not prove that payments work.
