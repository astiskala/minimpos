Prepare products, pricing and staff access

Connect payments first with Getting started. Then check these before adding your catalog.

Prices and tax

In Settings › Payments, choose a currency your Adyen account accepts; Automatic follows the device's region. In Settings › Tax, check the rates, whether prices include tax or add it at checkout, and the default rate for new products and custom items. Every product has a rate; use 0% for untaxed items. Switching off Charge tax stops charging tax but keeps the rates.

Changing currency or tax style asks for confirmation. A currency change keeps numeric prices, rounded to the new currency's precision—it does not perform an exchange-rate conversion. Review the preview and check product prices afterward.

Add products

Open Products, tap + and enter a name, price and tax rate. Add a category or barcode if useful; barcodes can be typed or scanned. The Categories tab groups the sale grid. Mark deposits as Pre-authorization products to keep them separate from ordinary sales.

Control staff access

In Settings › Security, set an admin PIN to protect Settings and Products, and choose the automatic re-lock time. Optionally set a separate Manager PIN for refunds, cancellations, captures and adjustments, including entering tips. Set the admin PIN first; removing it also removes the Manager PIN. Repeated incorrect attempts temporarily lock PIN entry.

Take a sale

  1. On Home, tap New sale. Tap products, scan barcodes or add a custom item.
  2. Tap Charge. Review the items, tax and total. Complete any references or email fields your business has enabled.
  3. Tap Pay and follow the terminal or Payments app. Wait for the result before starting another payment.
  4. Deliver the receipt from the result screen, or let automatic printing/emailing handle it.

A new setup asks only for payment; references, saved cards and tips are optional. Use a payment link if the customer should pay on their own phone instead.

An unknown result does not mean the payment failed. Check the original payment before charging again.

Refund a payment

  1. Tap Refund on Home and scan the receipt's refund QR code, or open the original sale in History.
  2. Choose a full refund, an amount or individual items. Item refunds require the original sale on this device; a receipt from another device supports full or amount refunds.
  3. Confirm the refund and obtain Manager PIN approval if configured.

Refunds are always linked to an original payment; unreferenced refunds are not supported. Refund a charged payment, but cancel an uncaptured deposit. Paid payment links must be refunded in the Customer Area.

Refund requested means Adyen received the request, not that the shopper has received the money. Check the final outcome in the Customer Area; Mini mPOS has no server to receive webhooks.

Find a payment and review totals

History groups payments and refunds by day. Use the filter chips for sales, Awaiting tip, pre-authorizations, refunds or Needs attention, and the Payment method menu for a card brand or wallet.

Search by merchant or PSP reference, authorization code, shopper reference or email, card last four digits, brand, wallet or exact amount. On a small screen, tap the search icon first. Every word must match: visa 34 finds Visa payments for 34 in the displayed currency. Refunds can also match their sale's shopper and card details.

Daily totals cover the transactions currently shown, including your search and filters. Unpaid links count nowhere; uncaptured deposits are Held, not sales revenue. History covers only records retained on this device, not your whole Adyen account.

Print, email or share receipts

From a result or History entry, use Print receipt or Email receipt. Tablets and phones also offer Share receipt, an image sent through Android's share sheet. Printing requires a connected terminal with a physical printer; Tap to Pay has none.

Customize and test

In Settings › Receipts, set your business details, header and footer, references and refund QR code. Choose automatic printing and when to print a merchant copy. The preview helps with layout; always use Print test receipt to check the actual printer.

Show tax amounts and Show taxable totals by rate are independent. To identify items at a particular rate, set Marked tax rate (%), an Item marker and its explanation. Leave the rate blank for no marking. These options work in every language and currency.

Language and receipt text

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. On Android 12 or earlier it follows the device language; on Android 13 or later use Android's per-app language setting. Adyen's payment receipt lines follow the terminal's own receipt-language setting and stay as supplied. Test the printed layout, especially for Chinese and Japanese text.

Set up email

Open Settings › Email. Choose a provider or enter your SMTP server, sender and credentials. Use STARTTLS or SSL, and send a test email. In Settings › Payments › Email receipts, choose when to ask for the customer's email and whether to send automatically.

Provider buttons fill in server details, not account authorization. Follow your provider's instructions for SMTP access and the required password:

  • Gmail
  • Microsoft 365
  • iCloud Mail
  • Yahoo Mail
  • Fastmail
  • Zoho Mail
  • Proton Mail

The app uses password-based SMTP, not OAuth. Provider or organization policy may prevent a connection even with the correct server details.

References and saved cards

Use Settings › Payments to enable a transaction reference if staff need to record a booking or order number. Separately, choose a shopper reference: none, a customer reference typed at checkout, or the customer's email. That shopper reference goes to Adyen with every payment, whether or not the card is saved. Email references are hashed by default; use the same salt across devices for consistent identifiers.

With a shopper reference configured, Offer to save cards makes card saving available at checkout. Obtain the shopper's consent and choose the recurring processing model appropriate to your Adyen integration. Saving a card does not provide a saved-card charging screen in Mini mPOS.

Let the customer pay with a link

Adyen Pay by Link lets the customer pay on Adyen's payment page instead of the terminal. Check your account's Pay by Link requirements, including terms and conditions, before LIVE use.

  1. Enable Settings › Payments › Offer payment links and choose the lifetime.
  2. At a sale's checkout, tap Create payment link. Show the QR code, Email link with the unpaid receipt, Print link, or Share link from a tablet or phone.
  3. Keep the link screen open for automatic checks, or tap Check payment later from its History entry. Use Cancel link when it is no longer needed.

History shows Awaiting payment until the link is paid, expires or is canceled. A paid link counts in totals; an unpaid one does not. Links include the items and any shopper details/card-saving choice. Your Adyen account's online risk and capture settings apply.

Links are for sales only, not deposits or tips on the receipt, and require the real Checkout API. They are not simulated. Anyone with the link can pay until it expires. Refund paid links in Customer Area › Payments › Payment list: the app cannot retrieve their PSP reference.

Hold and collect a deposit

A pre-authorization holds money on a card without charging it immediately. Ask Adyen Support to enable it for your payment methods; availability depends on the card and your business. See Adyen's pre-authorization guide. You do not need to change your whole account to manual capture: Mini mPOS requests it for these payments only.

  1. Tap New pre-authorization on Home. Choose a pre-authorization product, scan its barcode or enter a custom amount. Each pre-authorization contains one item.
  2. At checkout, tap Pre-authorize. The receipt shows the amount held.
  3. Open the entry in History when you are ready to collect or release it.
Capture
Collect the amount owed. A lower amount releases the remainder; a higher amount first asks the issuer to increase the authorization. Once capture is requested, refunds are available.
Adjust amount
Change the amount held without charging it. Submitting the same amount requests an authorization renewal.
Cancel pre-authorization
Release the hold. If the payment was already captured in the Customer Area, Adyen refunds it in full instead.

Captures and adjustments use the Checkout API. Capture requested is not final confirmation; check the Customer Area. To receive immediate authorization-adjustment answers, enable Return adjust authorisation data as described in Adyen's adjustment guide; otherwise confirmation is asynchronous.

Holds expire. Review the pre-authorization filter and capture or cancel promptly. For failed or unknown operations, use the recovery instructions.

Collect a tip written on the receipt

This needs a physical printer and Adyen's tipping-on-the-receipt setup. Ask Adyen Support to enable pre-authorization and overcapture for your payment methods. Some debit cards and wallets do not support this flow.

  1. At a sale's checkout, switch on Tip on the receipt and tap Pay. The bill is held, not captured.
  2. Print the receipt with blank Tip and Total lines. The merchant copy includes a signature line.
  3. After the customer fills it in, choose Enter tip from the result or History's Awaiting tip filter. Enter the tip or total, or choose No tip, and confirm.

The app requests capture of bill plus tip. Tips up to 20% use overcapture; larger tips first raise the authorization. If the issuer refuses that increase, the tip is not saved and you can enter a smaller one or none. Once saved, a tip cannot be changed. Before entering it, cancel the held payment rather than refund it; afterward refunds include the tip.

Enter tips promptly, including zero tips, so the bills are captured before their holds expire. A capture request still needs final confirmation in the Customer Area.

Set up more devices and manage local data

  1. On the configured device, open Settings › Data › Share with another device, or the Products menu. Choose the catalog, settings and/or passwords and keys, then Show QR codes.
  2. On the receiving device, choose Set up from another device and scan all codes in any order. Choose whether to merge or replace its catalog.
  3. To import secrets, enter the sender's transfer code. Leaving it blank skips secrets; an incorrect code imports nothing. Tap Import.
  4. Review prices and tax, check the connection in Settings › Terminal, and test a receipt.

Settings replace the receiving device's shared settings. Its payment destination, terminal address and ID, Tap to Pay installation, detected environment and simulator remain local. Included secrets cover API keys, the shared-key passphrase, SMTP password and both PINs. A shared key works for another terminal only if Adyen assigns it the same key; device-specific credentials may need replacing.

Check the import's currency warning: catalog prices are not exchange-rate converted. The setup helper is different: it imports only the connection fields you filled in, leaving unrelated settings alone.

This is a copy, not synchronization or a transaction-history backup. Future changes and each device's payments stay local. In Settings › Data, choose history retention and clear settled history when appropriate. Unresolved payments are protected from routine cleanup. Clearing Android app data removes all local products, settings and history.

Keep the app on the latest release. Install updates through the same route and signing source as the original installation; see installation.
