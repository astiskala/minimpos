Prepare products, tax and staff access

Before you take real payments, complete the Adyen connection setup. You need a payment terminal or the Adyen Payments app. Then prepare your products, tax and staff access once.

Set the currency and tax

  1. In Settings › Payments, choose a Currency that your merchant account accepts for in-person payments.
  2. In Settings › Tax, check the rates, whether prices include tax or tax is added at checkout, and the default rate for new products.

Every product has a tax rate; use a 0% rate for untaxed items. Switching off Charge tax stops charging tax but keeps the rates.

Important

Changing the currency keeps the numbers in your prices; it does not convert them at an exchange rate. Check your product prices afterward.

Add products

  1. Open Products and tap +.
  2. Enter a name, price and tax rate. Optionally add a category and a barcode, typed or scanned.
  3. For a deposit, switch on Pre-authorization product so that it appears only in pre-authorizations.

Set staff PINs

  1. In Settings › Security, tap Set PIN. It protects Settings and Products. Choose how soon the app locks again.
  2. Optionally, tap Set Manager PIN. Staff then need it for refunds, cancellations, captures, amount adjustments and entering tips.

To replace or remove the admin PIN, use Change Admin PIN or Remove Admin PIN; removing it also removes the Manager PIN. Repeated wrong attempts lock PIN entry for a while. For a forgotten PIN, see PIN problems.

Check and configure Adyen capabilities

Open Settings › Adyen capabilities after connecting your payment destination. The screen shows features as Available, Needs configuration or Unsupported on this destination. These are setup indicators, not proof that your merchant account, card or payment method can complete a transaction. Until payments are set up, the screen offers Finish setting up payments instead.

  1. Tap Refresh capabilities to read the selected terminal's settings. Real terminal inspection uses the Management API roles in Quick start.
  2. For supported terminals, configure terminal tipping for the payment currency, DCC, MOTO or offline limits. These controls update terminal settings at Adyen, not just Mini mPOS, and only when you tap Save to Adyen. Amounts labeled major units use whole currency units; minor-unit amounts use Adyen's currency precision.
  3. Enable the checkout options you need under Payments, Tips and donations or Shopper terminal display in Settings, then test them in TEST. The matrix also lists Adyen Giving, partial authorization, surcharge, gift cards, terminal reconciliation and shopper display; it does not provide an editor for every feature.

Tap to Pay does not offer physical-terminal features such as terminal tipping, shopper display, MOTO/MKE, offline payments or terminal reconciliation. Partial authorization is a separate payment option. For account enablement and payment-method requirements, follow the Adyen in-person payments documentation. Simulator availability does not establish real-terminal support.

Take a card payment

  1. On Home, tap New sale. Tap products, tap Scan barcode, or add a Custom item.
  2. Tap Charge. Check the items, tax and total, and fill in any reference or email fields.
  3. Tap Pay. The terminal, or the Adyen Payments app with Tap to Pay, asks the customer to pay.
  4. Wait for the result. Do not start another payment until it appears.
  5. Give the customer a receipt with Print receipt, Email receipt or Share receipt, unless it was delivered automatically.

Note

The terminal reads the card; Mini mPOS never receives full card details. Depending on the terminal and your merchant account, customers can insert or tap a card, or tap a phone or watch. For contactless payments, the card or phone must stay near the contactless symbol until the terminal confirms. With Tap to Pay, follow the instructions in the Adyen Payments app.

Warning

Result unknown does not mean the payment failed. Check the original payment before charging again.

Customer references, card saving and tips are optional; see references and saved cards. To let customers pay on their own phone, use a payment link. If a payment is declined, see declined payments.

Use optional checkout workflows

Enable only the checkout options you need in Settings. Except for partial authorization, these workflows need a supported physical terminal, not Tap to Pay. Check Adyen capabilities and test your actual setup first.

Collect a partially authorized payment

Turn on Allow partial authorizations to accept a card approval for less than the requested amount. When the result shows Partially paid, check the paid and remaining amounts, then tap Pay remaining on that result screen and collect the balance on another card. The app keeps the attempts on the same sale. Do not treat the sale as fully paid until the whole amount is approved.

A missing answer is not a partial approval. Resolve unknown results before collecting more. Do not start a new sale for the full original total: an approved portion has already been paid.

Use the shopper-facing terminal screen

With a separate network/cloud terminal, open Settings › Shopper terminal display. Choose whether to show item details before payment, ask the shopper to confirm the order, or collect an email when checkout has none. Asking for an email needs email receipts to be set up; the receipt is then emailed automatically when Email the receipt automatically is on. After approval, you can show a QR code and ask for a 0–10 rating; a returned rating appears in transaction details.

These prompts use the selected app language and are also simulated. They are not offered when Mini mPOS runs on the payment terminal itself. The displayed QR code contains the merchant reference, not a hosted receipt download; continue to print, email or share the receipt.

Invite a charity donation

After arranging Adyen Giving with Adyen, enable Charity donation (Adyen Giving) in Settings › Tips and donations. At sale checkout, select it to invite a donation on the terminal after payment. It is not offered for pre-authorizations or receipt-tip holds.

The purchase and donation are separate. A reported donation amount appears in transaction details; an approved purchase alone does not confirm a donation.

Take a MOTO or Manual Key Entry payment

  1. Check with Adyen that your terminal and account allow the required entry mode. Configure MOTO in Adyen capabilities when needed, and set staff PINs under Staff PINs.
  2. In Settings › Payments, enable the MOTO or Manual Key Entry option. At checkout, select Mail / telephone order (MOTO) for a remote order, or Manual Key Entry (MKE) when chip or contactless reading fails. Only one can be selected.
  3. Tap Pay, review the fraud and chargeback liability warning, and complete Manager approval when prompted. Enter card details on the terminal, never in Mini mPOS.

Keyed transactions carry higher fraud and chargeback liability for the merchant. Enabling an app switch does not enable the service at Adyen or remove that risk.

Recognize offline payments and pending batches

Offline EMV and Store-and-Forward need a supported terminal reached locally: Mini mPOS on that terminal or a terminal on your network. They are not offered through cloud routing or Tap to Pay. Arrange offline processing with Adyen and review its risks before setting limits in Adyen capabilities; Mini mPOS does not queue payments itself or make a terminal reachable when your local network is down.

History marks reported offline sales. Open a sale to see Offline processing, whether it used offline EMV or Store-and-Forward, and any Unconfirmed offline batches reported by the terminal. An offline approval can have no PSP reference yet. It is not an unknown result, but it does not confirm that Adyen has received or settled the payment.

Restore the terminal's connection to Adyen and verify uploaded payments and final outcomes in the Customer Area. Check the offline and unconfirmed-batch warnings during terminal reconciliation; matching totals do not confirm offline settlement. If no conclusive payment answer arrived, follow unknown-result recovery instead of assuming offline approval or charging again.

Scan a shopper wallet code

Use Scan wallet at sale checkout to read a payment code from the shopper’s wallet app. This is different from scanning a product barcode or a refund receipt. It works with a physical payment terminal, not Tap to Pay; wallet discovery needs a read role.

  1. Check the total and checkout fields, then tap Scan wallet. If receipt tipping is selected, confirm paying without it: the wallet charges the total immediately, not a pre-authorization.
  2. Choose the shopper’s wallet. A single available wallet is selected automatically. Only individually configured wallets appear; Alipay+ does not imply separate GCash, DANA, Kakao Pay or TrueMoney choices.
  3. On a confirmed scanner-equipped terminal, press its scan button. This may be the network/cloud terminal, not the device running Mini mPOS. To scan with the device’s camera instead, tap Use camera.
  4. Scan the shopper’s payment QR code or payment barcode. One valid scan starts payment immediately. Wait for the normal payment result and deliver its receipt.

Canceling keeps the cart and checkout fields. Invalid codes do not pay: scan again or change wallet. A native scan expires after 30 seconds; use Scan again or the camera. Leaving the app stops scanning; returning requires an explicit new scan. Codes cannot be typed or pasted.

Save card requests best-effort saving when shopper reference and consent allow it. Wallet support varies; only a returned stored-payment token means details were saved. An approved payment without a token is still approved. Handle wallet discovery or payment problems; never replace an unknown payment by scanning again.

Confirm your wallet setup and test behavior with the Adyen wallet guide and Adyen PayPal and Venmo guide.

Refund or cancel a payment

Refund
For a charged payment. Returns all or part of the money.
Cancel (void)
For a held payment that has not been captured: tap Cancel pre-authorization for a deposit, or Cancel payment for a sale Awaiting tip. This releases the hold.

To refund:

  1. On Home, tap Refund and scan the refund QR code on the receipt, or open the original sale in History.
  2. Choose a full refund, an amount or individual items. Item refunds need the original sale on this device.
  3. Confirm the refund, and enter the Manager PIN if asked.

Every refund is linked to its original payment; refunds without one are not supported. Refund paid payment links in the Customer Area.

Important

Refund requested means Adyen received the request, not that the customer has the money. Check the final outcome in the Customer Area. If a refund's result is unknown, check it before retrying.

Print, email or share receipts

From a payment result or a History entry, tap Print receipt or Email receipt. Tablets and phones also offer Share receipt, which sends an image through Android's share menu. Printing needs a terminal with a printer.

Customize and test receipts

  1. In Settings › Receipts, enter your business details, header and footer. Or tap Import business details from Adyen to review details from Adyen. Choose Fill missing details to keep existing text, or Replace existing details to use the supplied business name, address and phone. Blank imported fields, tax ID, title and footer stay unchanged.
  2. Choose Print automatically, when to print a Merchant copy, and whether to Show refund QR code.
  3. Tap Print test receipt and check the printout. The on-screen preview does not show the printer's exact layout.

Other options:

Show tax amounts
Shows the tax included in, or added to, the total. It does not change the tax charged.
Show tax breakdown
Lists the tax for each rate. Needed to show included tax when prices include tax.
Show taxable totals by rate
Groups item totals by tax rate, including 0%.
Show references
On by default. Prints the merchant reference and any customer reference, so that a receipt can be matched to its payment.
Marked tax rate (%)
Marks items at that rate with an Item marker and its explanation. Leave it blank for no marker.
Characters per line
32 by default, for narrow receipt rolls. Choose the width for your printer with a test print.

Set the language

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish and French. Choose Settings › Language to override the language on this device. The change applies immediately to app text and app-generated receipt labels; saved merchant text, product names, currency and tax settings stay unchanged. Follow device uses the device language on Android 12 or earlier, or Android's per-app language choice on Android 13 or later. Adyen's card receipt lines follow the terminal's own receipt language.

Set up email receipts

  1. Open Settings › Email receipts. Choose your provider, or enter the SMTP server, port, sender and login. Use STARTTLS or SSL/TLS.
  2. Send a test email.
  3. On the same screen, choose when to ask for the customer's email and whether to send receipts automatically.

Follow your provider's instructions to allow SMTP and get the right password:

  • Gmail
  • Microsoft 365
  • iCloud Mail
  • Yahoo Mail
  • Fastmail
  • Zoho Mail
  • Proton Mail

If receipts do not print or send, see printer and email problems.

Find payments and close the day

History lists payments and refunds by day, with daily totals.

Up to 100 matching entries across seven days are shown per page. Daily totals include the whole filtered day, even when its entries span pages. Use Previous and Next to browse pages. Search covers all retained transactions.

  • Filter by sales, Awaiting tip, pre-authorizations, refunds or Needs attention, and use Payment method for a card brand or wallet.
  • Search by reference, authorization code, customer reference or email, the card's last four digits, brand, wallet or exact amount. Every word must match: visa 34 finds Visa payments of 34.

On-screen totals follow your search and filters. Held payments, such as deposits and tips on the receipt, count as sales on the day their capture is accepted.

Close the day

  1. Open History, filter by Needs attention and resolve each entry; see unknown results.
  2. Check the Awaiting tip and pre-authorization filters. Enter tips, and capture or cancel deposits before their holds expire.
  3. Tap the print icon beside the day to print the Local daily summary.
  4. Reconcile with Adyen's reports in the Customer Area; see the Adyen settlement reconciliation guide.

The summary covers the whole day, ignoring filters: sales, tips included, accepted refunds, net, new holds and unresolved operations, for each currency. TEST and simulator activity is labeled. Paid payment links and captures without a known date are listed separately as undated activity.

Important

The Local daily summary covers only payments kept on this device. It is not Adyen's settlement and does not include other devices. The Customer Area is authoritative.

Warning

Switching between LIVE, TEST and the simulator permanently deletes all payment history and report data on this device. If unfinished payments exist, the app warns again: nothing is canceled at Adyen, but the local recovery and safe retry data is lost. Products and settings stay.

Compare with terminal totals and close a period

  1. On Home, tap Reconciliation, then Check terminal totals. Mini mPOS first checks pending or unknown terminal payments, then shows Mini mPOS and terminal sales and refunds side by side.
  2. If Totals differ, resolve the payments that need attention in History and check again. The terminal period is not necessarily a calendar day; other devices using the terminal or missing local history can cause differences. Payment links, token charges and sample history are not terminal payments.
  3. At the end of the day, tap Close terminal period, confirm, and complete Manager approval if requested. The terminal ends its period and starts a new one; this cannot be undone.
  4. Optionally, tap Print today’s report for the Local daily summary.

This is available for physical terminals and the simulator, not Tap to Pay. A matching comparison or closed period does not confirm settlement, refund completion or offline upload. Keep using the Customer Area for final financial outcomes.

Use references and save cards

Merchant reference
To record a booking or order number, turn on Settings › Payments › Ask for a merchant reference. The number identifies the payment in Adyen. If left blank, a unique reference is generated.
Shopper reference
In Settings › Payments, choose the Shopper reference: a customer reference typed at checkout (the default), the customer's email, or none. It is sent to Adyen with every payment. Email references are hashed by default; use the same salt on every device.
Saved cards
With a shopper reference set, turn on Offer to save cards and choose the Recurring processing model for your Adyen setup. Get the customer's consent before selecting Save card for future payments. To charge one later, see charge a token.

Charge a token

  1. On Home, tap Charge token and enter the Manager PIN if asked.
  2. Enter the customer reference, or the email when that is the shopper reference, and tap Find tokens.
  3. Choose a token, then add products or a Custom item. This cart is separate from New sale.
  4. Review the charge and tap Charge.

The customer is not present, so get their consent first. Tokens saved for a different Recurring processing model cannot be chosen. Refund these payments from History; their receipts have no refund QR code. If a charge's result is unknown, check it from History within seven days, after that in the Customer Area. At a New sale’s checkout, once the entered shopper reference has tokens saved, Charge token appears between Scan wallet and Create payment link: after Manager approval, choose a token to charge the sale to it.

Take payment with a link

Prerequisites

  • Your merchant account meets the Adyen Pay by Link requirements, including terms and conditions, before live use
  1. At a sale's checkout, tap Create payment link.
  2. Show the QR code, or tap Email link, Print link or, on a tablet or phone, Share link.
  3. Keep the link screen open to see the payment arrive, or later tap Check payment in its History entry.
  4. If the link is no longer needed, tap Cancel link.

The entry shows Awaiting payment until the link is paid, expires or is canceled. Set how long links work in Settings › Payments › Payment links. Links are for sales only, not deposits or tips on the receipt.

In the simulator, links work offline without API credentials. Their QR codes cannot take payments. Tap Simulate payment to complete the demo, with Manager approval if configured. The screen and delivered receipts remain labeled as demos; no money moves.

Important

Anyone with a real link can pay until it expires. Refund paid links in Customer Area › Payments › Payment list; the app cannot refund them.

If a link is missing or not updating, see payment link problems.

Hold and collect a deposit

Prerequisites

  • Pre-authorization enabled by Adyen Support for your payment methods; see the Adyen pre-authorization guide

A pre-authorization holds an amount on the card without charging it. Your whole account does not need manual capture; Mini mPOS requests it for these payments only.

  1. On Home, tap New pre-authorization. Choose a pre-authorization product, scan its barcode or enter an amount. Each pre-authorization has one item.
  2. At checkout, tap Pre-authorize. The receipt shows the amount held.
  3. When you are ready, open the entry in History and choose an action:
Capture
Collects the amount owed. A lower amount releases the rest; a higher amount first asks the card issuer to increase the hold. Refunds are possible once capture is requested.
Adjust amount
Changes the amount held without charging it. Submitting the same amount renews the authorization.
Cancel pre-authorization
Releases the hold. If the payment was already captured in the Customer Area, Adyen refunds it in full instead.

Capture requested is not final confirmation; check the Customer Area. For immediate answers to adjustments, enable Return adjust authorisation data as described in the Adyen authorization adjustment guide.

Warning

Holds expire. Check the pre-authorization filter daily and capture or cancel promptly. For failed or unknown operations, see capture problems.

Collect a tip on the terminal

  1. Check terminal tipping in Adyen capabilities. Set the terminal's predefined and custom tip options for the payment currency when needed.
  2. In Settings › Tips and donations, enable Tip on the terminal and choose its checkout default. Optionally enable Dynamic basket tip suggestions for fixed amounts on small baskets and percentages on larger ones, or Tip on the pre-tax amount.
  3. At sale checkout, select Tip on the terminal and tap Pay. The shopper chooses a tip before the card is charged. Check the returned total and tip and deliver the receipt.

This does not need a printer or later tip entry. Terminal tipping and tips on the receipt cannot be selected together. Neither is offered for pre-authorizations, scanned-wallet payments or payment links; terminal tipping is not offered with Tap to Pay.

Collect a tip written on the receipt

Prerequisites

  • A terminal with a printer
  • Pre-authorization and overcapture enabled by Adyen Support; see the Adyen receipt tipping guide

In Settings › Tips and donations, choose Enabled (default off) or Enabled (default on) to set how the checkout switch starts. Disabled, the default, hides it. Some debit cards and wallets do not support this flow.

  1. At a sale's checkout, switch on Tip on the receipt and tap Pay. The bill is held, not charged.
  2. Print the receipt. It has blank Tip and Total lines, and the merchant copy has a signature line.
  3. When the customer has filled it in, tap Enter tip on the result or in History's Awaiting tip filter.
  4. Enter the tip or the total, or tap No tip, and confirm.

The app captures the bill plus the tip. Tips over 20% first need the card issuer to increase the hold; if it refuses, enter a smaller tip or none. A saved tip cannot be changed. Before a tip is entered, cancel the payment instead of refunding it; afterward, refunds include the tip.

Warning

Enter every tip promptly, including zero tips, so the bills are captured before their holds expire. For failed or unknown captures, see capture problems.

Set up more devices and manage data

  1. On the configured device, open Settings › Data and transfer › Share with another device. Choose the catalog, settings and/or passwords and keys, then tap Show QR codes.
  2. On the new device, choose Set up from another device and scan all the codes, in any order. Choose whether to merge or replace its catalog.
  3. Enter the 12-character transfer code shown on the first device, even when copying only products or settings. Keep it separate from the QR codes. Tap Import.
  4. Check prices and tax, test the connection in Settings › Terminal, and print a test receipt.

The new device keeps its own payment destination, terminal, environment and Tap to Pay registration. A shared key works for another terminal only if Adyen assigns it the same key.

Important

This is a one-time copy, not synchronization or a backup of payment history. Each device keeps its own payments. Clearing the app's data in Android deletes all products, settings and history on the device.

In Settings › Data and transfer, set Keep transactions for or tap Clear settled history; unresolved payments are always kept.

On a tablet or phone, Mini mPOS checks for a newer version at startup. If Home offers Update, tap it to open the APK in your browser, then install the download. Adyen terminals update through the Customer Area, not this prompt. You can also get the latest release and follow the installation steps. Keep the same signing source and install over the existing app; do not uninstall or clear its data.

For import problems, see PIN and transfer problems.
